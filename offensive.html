02 / Penetration testing lifecycle

From scope to remediation

Each phase creates context for the next while preserving authorization, evidence quality, and safe lab boundaries.

  1. 01

    Planning & Pre-Engagement

    Define the authorized scope, rules of engagement, success criteria, communication paths, and handling requirements before testing begins.

    • Scope and asset confirmation
    • Rules of engagement
    • Authorization and safety constraints
    • Test objectives and schedule
    Case study coming soon
  2. 02

    Reconnaissance & OSINT

    Identify externally observable information before active interaction with the target.

    • Domain and DNS research
    • Certificate transparency review
    • Technology fingerprinting
    • Public infrastructure discovery
    • Organizational OSINT
    Case study coming soon
  3. 03

    Scanning & Enumeration

    Use controlled active testing to identify reachable services, application paths, identities, and configuration details.

    • Port and service discovery
    • Directory and endpoint enumeration
    • Protocol and banner analysis
    • Authenticated surface review
    Case study coming soon
  4. 04

    Vulnerability Analysis

    Correlate technical observations, configuration weaknesses, and business context to determine which findings warrant validation.

    • Manual validation
    • False-positive reduction
    • Exploitability assessment
    • Risk and impact analysis
    Case study coming soon
  5. 05

    Exploitation

    Safely validate material weaknesses within the agreed rules, collecting enough evidence to demonstrate impact without unnecessary disruption.

    • Proof-of-concept validation
    • Access control testing
    • Privilege boundary checks
    • Evidence capture and rollback
    Case study coming soon
  6. 06

    Post-Exploitation

    Assess the practical consequences of access by examining persistence, privilege expansion, data exposure, and detection opportunities.

    • Privilege escalation review
    • Identity and trust path analysis
    • Controlled data access validation
    • Detection and cleanup checks
    Case study coming soon
  7. 07

    Reporting & Remediation

    Turn validated evidence into prioritized findings, actionable fixes, executive context, and a clear path to retesting.

    • Technical finding write-ups
    • Risk prioritization
    • Remediation recommendations
    • Retest planning and closure
    Case study coming soon

03 / Tools & techniques

Tools support the method

The objective determines the technique; the technique determines the tool.

Reconnaissance

Asset discovery, OSINT correlation, technology identification, and attack surface mapping.

  • Passive enumeration
  • Service discovery
  • Metadata review

Web & API Testing

Request analysis and manual validation focused on access control, input handling, and application logic.

  • Authentication testing
  • Authorization testing
  • Input validation

Network & Identity

Protocol analysis and privilege-path review across network services and identity environments.

  • Service enumeration
  • Trust relationship review
  • Privilege boundary analysis

Automation & Evidence

Lightweight scripting and repeatable evidence collection to improve consistency and reduce avoidable noise.

  • Python scripting
  • Structured notes
  • Reproducible validation

04 / Reporting & remediation

Technical testing only creates value when findings can be understood and acted upon.

Reporting connects technical evidence to decisions. A clear finding should move from concise context to defensible evidence, business impact, and a practical remediation path.

  • Evidence firstClear reproduction details and scoped supporting evidence.
  • Risk in contextSeverity informed by exploitability, exposure, and business impact.
  • Remediation with directionSpecific recommendations that can be assigned, prioritized, and retested.

Finding anatomy

A report structure built for action

  1. 01Executive SummaryWhat matters and why it matters.
  2. 02Technical FindingWhat was observed and validated.
  3. 03EvidenceScoped proof that supports the conclusion.
  4. 04Business ImpactThe consequence in operational context.
  5. 05RemediationA prioritized path toward risk reduction.
