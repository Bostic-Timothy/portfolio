Sample deliverable / Fictional organization

A plan leadership can act on.

A checkup translates observations into priorities, owners, and evidence of completion. Expand the examples to see the approach.

01 · Confirm who owns important accounts

Example observation: A fictional organization has no current administrator list. Next step: The office lead and authorized IT provider document named owners, access roles, and recovery contacts. Evidence: A reviewed access register with no passwords stored in it.

02 · Make payment verification routine

Example observation: The team has no written process for payment-detail changes. Next step: The finance lead defines approval responsibilities and independent verification using a previously known contact method. Evidence: An approved procedure practiced with a fictional request.

03 · Verify what can be recovered

Example observation: Backups are listed, but no restore-test record is available. Next step: The authorized backup provider schedules a controlled restoration of agreed test data. Evidence: A dated result, recovery limitations, and a named follow-up owner.

05 / Working together

Know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Start with the need

    Tell me what your team does and where technology slows things down.

  2. 02

    Agree on a useful scope

    Define the deliverables, cost, access, and any outside provider responsibilities.

  3. 03

    Review, act, and follow through

    Review the findings or training together, agree on action owners, and document the next steps. Ongoing support is scoped separately.

Do you provide 24/7 support or penetration testing?

I do not personally provide 24/7 coverage or commercial penetration testing. The launch focuses on readiness, training, and technology operations. Continuous monitoring, emergency incident response, complex infrastructure, and formal assessments require qualified providers and explicit agreements.

Can you work with a church’s existing systems?

We can start by discussing your current process. Any integration depends on the platform’s supported features, your permissions, and a scope I can deliver. A small reporting tool does not replace a church management or giving platform.

What will a project cost?

Cost depends on the agreed scope. We’ll discuss deliverables and outside expenses before any paid work begins. Vendor subscriptions and specialist services are separate unless explicitly included in a written agreement.
