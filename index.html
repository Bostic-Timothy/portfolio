03 / The person behind the work

Service is the thread.
Technology is the next chapter.

Timothy (T.J.) Bostic

A commitment to people,
carried into the work.

A heart for service has guided every career move I’ve made. I spent a decade in the U.S. Army, serving in the 82nd Airborne Infantry, the 2nd Infantry Division, and USAREC Recruiting. Those years shaped my sense of responsibility to the people who depend on me.

That same calling led me into the school system and, eventually, into work with the church. In the Winston-Salem area, serving students, families, and congregations has made community involvement a continuing part of my life. Teaching, encouraging others, and supporting the people around me are the work behind the résumé.

Now I’m bringing that commitment into cybersecurity and practical technology. Trust is earned through care, sound judgment, and follow-through. I want to be someone you can talk to plainly about your organization’s cyber needs—and someone who is honest about the help I can provide and when a specialist should step in.

05 / Working together

Know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Start with the need

    Tell me what your team does and where technology slows things down.

  2. 02

    Agree on a useful scope

    Define the deliverables, cost, access, and any outside provider responsibilities.

  3. 03

    Build, review, hand over

    Review the result together and leave with documentation and clear next steps.

Do you provide 24/7 support or penetration testing?

Those are not part of this launch offering. The security content here is learning and lab work. Managed monitoring, incident response, penetration testing, and compliance assessments require an appropriately qualified provider and a separate scope.

Can you work with a church’s existing systems?

We can start by discussing your current process. Any integration depends on the platform’s supported features, your permissions, and a scope I can deliver. A small reporting tool does not replace a church management or giving platform.

What will a project cost?

Cost depends on the agreed scope. We’ll discuss deliverables and outside expenses before any paid work begins. Vendor subscriptions and specialist services are separate unless explicitly included in a written agreement.
